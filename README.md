# Sales Performance Dashboard | Syntecxhub Internship

## Project Overview

This project involves the development of an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyse Superstore sales data covering 2019–2020.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of revenue, profitability, sales trends, regional performance, product categories, and individual product performance. It transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights to support performance monitoring and informed decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse monthly revenue trends over time.
- Evaluate revenue, profit, and profit margin.
- Measure year-on-year revenue and profit growth.
- Compare sales performance across product categories and regions.
- Identify the top five and bottom five products by revenue.
- Present key performance indicators in an interactive dashboard.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** Calculation of performance measures and key performance indicators.

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes six key performance indicators:

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Revenue Growth (YoY)
- Profit Growth (YoY)
- Total Orders

It also contains five analytical visualizations:

1. **Monthly Revenue Trend:** Tracks changes in monthly revenue across the reporting period.
2. **Revenue and Profit by Category:** Compares financial performance across product categories.
3. **Revenue and Profit by Region:** Evaluates revenue contribution and profitability across regions.
4. **Top 5 Best-Selling Products:** Highlights the products generating the highest revenue.
5. **Bottom 5 Low-Performing Products:** Identifies the products generating the lowest revenue.

## Key Business Insights

The analysis produced the following findings:

- **Revenue Growth:** Revenue increased by 77.29% year-on-year in 2020.
- **Profit Growth:** Profit increased by 14.2%, considerably below the reported revenue growth rate.
- **Category Performance:** Office Supplies generated the highest revenue, followed by Technology and Furniture.
- **Regional Performance:** The West recorded the highest revenue, while the South recorded the lowest.
- **Product Performance:** The dashboard identifies the five highest- and lowest-revenue-generating products for further evaluation.

These findings highlight the importance of examining profitability alongside revenue growth when evaluating business performance.

*Note: Growth figures should be interpreted in the context of the dataset's reporting period and the calculation methods used.*
